## 1. How seams and sheets work today

Frames. (1) uv: each panel's own development frame, mm. (2) boat-plan: the panel rotated and placed by its world centroid relative to the primary panel (layout.py:8, 144-150). (3) placed = nest: boat-plan plus a per-panel `nest_offset` for panels that collided (layout.py:194-197); on the AXIS run panels 2, 4 and 5 are moved. final_auto.dxf, seams.json, hover points, the boat axis, the snap reference pool and split_panel all live here, so nested panels are "near" each other at nest distances, not boat distances. The axis is run.json `teak.frame.longitudinal_axis` via `sheetjob.resolve_frame` (124-203); a grain override becomes `[cos, sin]`; across = (along_y, -along_x). (4) sheet: `sheet_transform(axis)` (sheets.py:633-649) rotates placed so the axis is +Y; `Placement.apply` (nesting.py:51-54) adds the 0/180 turn and the 12.7 mm usable-area offset. (5) bow-up screen: app.js:361-396 removes each panel's nest offset, flips y and rotates so the bow points up; without a bow the raw nested layout is shown.

Seam storage. `<run>/seams.json`, placed frame: `x1..y2` as cut, `raw` (the drawing), `mode` ("" / along / across / angle), `angle_deg`, `snap`, `panel_id`, `seam_id` (sheets.py:340-456). Old-schema files (this fixture) hold only ids, `panel_id: null` and x1..y2, read as mode "", raw None, snap True. The optimiser keeps `seams_previous.json`, rotating older copies to `seams_previous_N.json`.

UI actions. Hover: each pointer move (40 ms throttle, one in flight) posts `/api/seam/hover`; `bridge.seam_hover` (374-456) derives the master direction, `seamplace.seam_through` returns every chord >= 5 mm of the infinite line on every panel, `_settled` (542-589) slides each chord up to 25 mm onto a parallel fitted edge or saved seam within 300 mm and re-trims it; the page keeps the chord bracketing the pointer (2 mm grace) and shows "N mm on panel k"; the settle note is discarded. Click: `placeHovered` stores that one chord (panel_id, mode, snap true, raw = chord) and `replanSheets` posts the whole list plus `sheetSettings()` to `/api/sheets/seams`; `checked_seams`/`sheet_options` validate; `bridge.sheet_preview(save=True)` names id-less seams `s{i+1}`, runs `sheetjob.preview`, then writes the cut seams to seams.json. A reply is adopted whole only when its stamp `{rev, told, clean}` is current, otherwise merged per seam_id. Remove, "as placed" and Clear all use the same POST (clear posts []); the save happens only after the plan succeeds. Recalculate re-posts with the page's settings. Find the best seam layout posts `/api/seams/optimise` (busy-guarded): `bridge.job_optimise_seams` runs `seamplan.optimise` (90 s budget, proxy nests, top 12 arrangements confirmed through `sheetjob.plan`) and only if improved rotates the backup, copies seams.json to seams_previous.json and writes the result through `sheet_preview(save=True)`; the page polls `/api/jobs/<id>` every 700 ms. Undo fetches `seams_previous.json` via `/api/file` and posts it as an edit. Export posts `/api/sheets/export` (busy-guarded): `sheetjob.plan(write_files=True)` writes `sheet_NN.dxf`, `sheets.json` and `sheet_report.md` into the run folder, with `_refuse` as a last envelope gate (0.1 mm). Opening a run, the Sheets tab and every job completion GET `/api/sheets` (engine defaults, writes nothing), then POST again when settings are non-default.

Pipeline. `plan` (sheetjob.py:419-534): read seams; final.dxf beats final_auto.dxf (`source_dxf` 41-48); resolve the frame; `apply_snap` (253-312: a moded seam is re-aimed about its midpoint onto the current master then slid <= 25 mm; a mode "" seam gets axis capture <= 20 deg, the slide, else feature snapping); `split_panel` per panel (sheets.py:511-586: a seam is relevant if its panel_id matches or, when free, its drawn segment intersects; each relevant seam is extended by the bbox diagonal + 10 mm, buffered 3 mm and subtracted; parts under 400 mm2 are dropped with a count-only warning; pieces are named `P<n>-<rank by area>` and their rings rebuilt by `_rebuild_ring`); `oversize_report`; `nesting.nest` (largest area first, first sheet with a spot, y then x on a 5 mm grid, 0 then 180, occupancy = placed pieces buffered 20 mm). `preview` (537-609) is `plan(write_files=False)` plus screen rings re-split from `result["seams"]`, and `files` = whatever `sheet_*.dxf` is on disk. Preview and cut therefore always agree with each other, including when both are wrong.

Files. seams.json: every successful seam POST, optimiser write, undo. seams_previous(_N).json: optimiser only. sheet_NN.dxf, sheets.json, sheet_report.md: export only; older sheet files are never removed. GET /api/sheets and run.json: never written by this pipeline.
